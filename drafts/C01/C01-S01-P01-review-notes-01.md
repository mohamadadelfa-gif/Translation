## Key lexical decisions and confidence

### forced exodus → «کوچ اجباری»

**Confidence:** Contextual proposal; Ready for Adel's decision.

Why:
- `exodus` is broader than formal `expulsion`;
- Hezareh gives the field of group departure/migration;
- فرهنگ طیفی places کوچ / مهاجرت / خروج together in the movement field;
- historical evidence shows the actual process included flight, evacuation, deportation and expulsion.

Do not narrow automatically to «اخراج» unless the English source says `expulsion` or the specific context requires it.

### concentration camp → «اردوگاه متمرکز»

**Confidence:** Historical terminology check completed; Persian candidate.

This keeps the generic category distinct from killing/death centers. The Persian USHMM terminology explicitly uses «اردوگاه‌های متمرکز» while noting that «اردوگاه کار اجباری» is common Persian usage but can blur categories.

### special concentration camps → «اردوگاه‌های مرگ»

**Confidence:** **Approved by Adel for this passage.**

This is contextual identification of the referent, not `special = مرگ`.

### mass exterminations → «کشتارهای جمعی»

**Confidence:** Contextual historical proposal.

The dictionaries place `extermination` in the field of destruction/eradication; the Holocaust context requires mass killing. «پاک‌سازی» is too weak.

### our shame lies open to the eyes of the world

Current proposal:

«شرم ما پیش چشم جهانیان عیان است»

**Confidence:** Contextual proposal.

The phrase preserves the visibility metaphor and fits formal Persian. Daryabandari provides prose evidence for natural «پیش چشم» construction.

### Humanity shudders in horror at Germany!

Current proposal:

«بشریت در برابر آلمان از وحشت به خود می‌لرزد!»

**Confidence:** Contextual proposal.

`at Germany` is non-locative; «در آلمان» is incorrect. فرهنگ طیفی's fear field directly includes «از ترس لرزیدن» / «به خود لرزیدن».

### Stunde Null

Candidate: «ساعت صفر» (*Stunde Null*)

Historical check: a postwar rupture metaphor/topos, not a neutral claim that all continuities actually disappeared.

### Nullpunkt

Candidate: «نقطهٔ صفر» (*Nullpunkt*)

Keep distinct from `Stunde Null` where Brockmann contrasts them.
