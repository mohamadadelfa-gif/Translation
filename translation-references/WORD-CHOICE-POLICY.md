## Core rule

Word choice follows the sequence defined in `MENTOR_WORKFLOW.md`:

```text
source meaning + local argument
→ English structure
→ bilingual / specialist evidence when materially relevant
→ Persian semantic-family and register exploration
→ Persian usage / orthography / prose reconstruction
→ Adel's decision
```

Do not create, preserve, or infer a parallel evidence gate in this file. If wording here ever conflicts with `MENTOR_WORKFLOW.md`, the workflow controls.
