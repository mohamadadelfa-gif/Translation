## Example lookup logic

For a sentence such as:

`Tracing the origins ..., one can discern ...`

the first likely checks are:

- Chapter 14 for the non-finite `-ing` clause;
- Chapter 8 for its possible adjunct/supplement function;
- Chapter 3 for modal `can`;
- Chapter 4 for the structure of the matrix clause.

This map only identifies likely places to inspect. It does not by itself establish the analysis of a particular sentence.
